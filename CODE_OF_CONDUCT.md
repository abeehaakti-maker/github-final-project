# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and kind to others.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Do not use offensive language or harass anyone.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project owner. The project owner may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.
